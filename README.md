# 🏆 Sports Products Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard developed in Power BI to monitor sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional sales distribution for a sports products retailer.

The dashboard provides business stakeholders with actionable insights into sales channels, product categories, retailer performance, and overall business growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to help management:

* Monitor sales, costs, and profit performance
* Track product sales and quantity sold
* Analyze sales channels effectiveness
* Evaluate retailer and regional performance
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Dashboard Features

### 📈 Sales Comparison (This Year vs Last Year)

Tracks monthly sales performance and compares current-year sales against the previous year.

**Insights:**

* Identify growth trends
* Compare year-over-year performance
* Detect seasonal sales patterns

---

### 🛒 Sales by Sales Channel

Analyzes revenue contribution across different sales channels:

* In-store
* Online
* Outlet

**Purpose:**

* Measure channel effectiveness
* Optimize sales strategies
* Improve revenue generation

---

### 📦 Product Performance Analysis

Provides detailed product-level performance metrics:

* Sales Revenue
* Quantity Sold
* Profit
* Profit Percentage

Supports analysis by:

* Men's Products
* Women's Products
* Product Categories

---

### 🗺️ Regional Sales Analysis

Visualizes sales distribution across states using geographic mapping.

**Purpose:**

* Identify high-performing regions
* Compare regional sales performance
* Support market expansion decisions

---

### 🏪 Retailer Analysis

Interactive filters enable analysis by:

* Retailer
* Product
* Region
* Month
* Year

Providing flexible drill-down capabilities.
